## ✦ What we do

### 01 · Platform & Apps
*Four ready-to-run products on one trusted stack, priced simply.*

| Product | What it is | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Floyd Worker** | Agentic process orchestrator: design, develop, deploy and govern agentic business workflows, with governance enforced at runtime | Any hyperscaler (AWS, Azure, GCP) · 50+ connectors · Runtime governance |
| **Floyd IQ** | Low/no-code data and analytics: custom analyst agents help business users analyse data, create reports and dashboards, and build predictive models | Analyst agents · Reports & dashboards · Predictive models |
| **Floyd Apps** | Workplace apps, ready in days: service management, customer and marketing operations, and billing and invoicing, customised for your business and built on Floyd Worker | No per-seat pricing · Unlimited users · Ready in days |
| **Floyd Assure** | Insurance for agent actions: underwrites the risk of an incorrect agent action causing a business loss | Patent pending · Risk-tiered premiums · Loss cover |
